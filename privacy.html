Awesome GSC Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Awesome GSC is an iPhone app that shows your Google Search Console data. We built it so that your data never touches our servers — because we don't have any.

What data Awesome GSC accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant Awesome GSC access to the Google Search Console API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters) and your email address. Awesome GSC uses this access only to:

  • list the Search Console properties you have access to;
  • show search performance data (clicks, impressions, CTR, position, keywords, pages, countries, devices);
  • run URL inspections you request;
  • list sitemaps and submit a sitemap when you tap “Submit”.

Your email address is only shown to you in Settings so you know which account is connected.

Where your data goes

All requests go directly from your iPhone to Google. Awesome GSC has no backend, does not collect analytics, does not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share any data. Your sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Search data is cached temporarily in memory, and a small summary (property name and click totals) is stored on your device for the Home Screen widget.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Awesome GSC's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is never transferred to anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Tap Settings › Sign out to revoke Awesome GSC's Google access and delete tokens from your device. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Awesome GSC is not directed at children under 13.
