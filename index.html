Questions

Do I need a Google Search Console account?

Yes. Awesome GSC shows data from your existing Search Console properties. If you don't have one yet, add your website at search.google.com/search-console first. You can also explore the app with sample data before signing in.

Does it work with domain properties?

Yes. Both domain properties (like example.com) and URL-prefix properties (like https://www.example.com/) are supported, and you can switch between all your properties in one tap.

What can Awesome GSC change in my Search Console?

Only one thing: it can submit a sitemap when you ask it to. Everything else is read-only. You can revoke access at any time from Settings in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Awesome GSC. You can also tap "Manage subscription" in the app's settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Is Awesome GSC made by Google?

No. Awesome GSC is an independent app made by Tuna Ventures Pty Ltd. It uses Google's official Search Console API with your permission.
